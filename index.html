Take the car off the patient. v0.7.0

Your readiness
What this is

Vehicle extrication, the engine crew's part: size it up, make it safe, stabilize, get in, and take the car off the patient while the EMS provider inside keeps them safe. You call it the way an officer calls it (“Peel the trim.” “Pop the latch.”), the crew does it, and every command carries a card that says why. Modeled on the vehicle chapter of NFPA 2500, in our own words. On a real call, follow your department's procedures and the maker's rescue guide.

Difficulty

Drill night

Live injects while a scenario runs (the patient crashes, a tool battery dies, a fuel leak, the car shifts; a second patient arrives with the two-car crash) and Freeze and discuss. On by itself during a Drill Night.
