Take the car off the patient. v0.1.0

Your readiness
What this is

Vehicle extrication at the Operations level: size it up, make it safe, stabilize, get in, and take the car off the patient while the EMS provider inside keeps them safe. Modeled on the vehicle chapter of NFPA 2500, in our own words. On a real call, follow your department's procedures and the maker's rescue guide.

Difficulty

Drill night

Live injects (the patient crashes, a tool battery dies, a fuel leak, the car shifts, a second patient) and a freeze while a scenario runs. On by itself during a Drill Night.
